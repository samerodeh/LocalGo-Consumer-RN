03How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To price, charge for, and deliver your orders.
  • To share the delivery address and your name with the driver who accepts your order — they cannot complete the delivery without it.
  • To send order-status notifications, if you allowed them.
  • To answer questions in the in-app assistant.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no ads or advertising trackers.

05Location

Location is optional and the app asks before using it. Granting it lets you fill in your current address with one tap instead of typing it. Decline, and you can still type an address and order normally — nothing else in the app depends on location.

We use location only at the moment you ask us to. The app does not track you in the background or record where you go.

06The in-app assistant

LocalGO includes an assistant that can search the menu, build your cart, and start checkout from a conversation. To answer you, it sends your messages — along with what is currently in your cart and your delivery address — to Groq, which runs the language model that writes the reply.

Please don't type anything into the assistant you wouldn't want processed by a third party. It only needs to know about food.

07How long we keep it

  • Account details — until you delete your account.
  • Orders — retained after an account is deleted, with your name, delivery address, and account link removed from them. We keep accurate business and tax records; we do not keep your identity attached to them.
  • Notification token — until you turn off notifications or delete your account.
  • Order and address history on your phone — stored on the device itself and removed when you uninstall the app.

08Security

Everything the app sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored by us in a readable form. Access to the order database is restricted per-account by row-level security rules, so one customer cannot read another's orders.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise otherwise.

09Your rights, and deleting your account

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it entirely. Under Québec's Law 25 and Canada's PIPEDA you also have the right to withdraw consent and to complain to a regulator.

To delete your account

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete Account. It takes effect immediately — no waiting period, no email required. This removes your account, your profile, your saved addresses and payment cards, your assistant chat history, and your notification token.

Past orders are kept as business records, with your name, address, and account link stripped off them, as described in section 7. Your own messages to a driver are deleted.

If you can no longer sign in, email YOUR CONTACT EMAIL from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my account", and we will do it for you within 30 days.

One thing outside our control: Stripe keeps its own record of payments it processed, for its own legal and accounting obligations. We cannot delete those, and neither can you — but they are held by Stripe under its privacy policy, not by us.

10Children

LocalGO is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will remove it.
